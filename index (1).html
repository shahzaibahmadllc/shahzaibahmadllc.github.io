About

The accountant who queries the database himself

I began my career in the accounts department at Al Sehar International in Faisalabad (Jan 2025 – May 2026) — processing vendor invoices, reconciling accounts, and closing the month. That's where I learned that most of an accountant's time goes into finding and fixing data problems, not accounting itself.

So I built the toolkit for it: advanced Excel for modeling and scenario forecasting, SQL for pulling and cleaning data at scale, and Power BI for turning monthly reporting into something management actually reads. In 2026 I completed the ICPAP Advanced and Post Graduate Diplomas in Public Accounting — both attested for Saudi Arabia — and a Financial Modeling & Valuation certification from IIM Skills.

I'm currently specializing in SAP Accounts Payable / P2P processes and building a 200,000-row AP analytics capstone. My goal is simple: be the person in the finance team who doesn't wait for a report — the one who builds it.
