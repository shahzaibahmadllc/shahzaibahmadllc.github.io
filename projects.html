Bank Fraud Detection Analysis Completed

SQL · Power BI · DAX

ProblemIdentify where fraud concentrates inside a large volume of banking transactions — by channel, authentication method, time of day, and account profile — and quantify the financial exposure.
BuiltA SQL analysis layer of 15+ queries: fraud-rate distribution, fraud by payment channel and authentication type, international-transaction and suspicious-IP breakdowns, average risk scores, peak fraud hours, account-age analysis, top-10 largest fraud transactions (DENSE_RANK), total fraud loss in millions, and CASE-based risk tiers (High / Medium / Low).
DeliveredA 3-page Power BI report: Executive Fraud Overview (KPI cards, fraud share donut, peak fraud activity hours, fraud by account age), Fraud Risk Analysis (channel and authentication-type breakdowns), and a Detailed Fraud Risk Matrix for case-level review.
ResultA repeatable screening workflow that turns raw transaction data into a ranked, reviewable fraud exposure report.
